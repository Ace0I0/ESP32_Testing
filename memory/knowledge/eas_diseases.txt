## Deep Root Disease
APSO-DRD-032
Name: Deep Root Disease / Deep Root Virus
Status: Active containment watch.
Category: Biological anomaly, parasitic root-stage infection, transformation hazard.
Known associations: Roots, bulbs, sprouts, vessel-stage infection, Nature's Mockery, Iris-linked growth signatures.
Activity summary: Reports describe root-like growth patterns, skin bulb formation, tissue discoloration, immobilization, altered recall, dream cessation, and late-stage conversion into nonstandard organic structures. Current Artemis records treat Deep Root Disease and Deep Root Virus as two labels for the same family of fictional root-linked hazards. Disease is used in older recovered tapes. Virus is used by newer Artemis internal files.
Primary uncertainty: It is not clear whether the root agent is biological, extraterrestrial, fungal, parasitic, or a composite of all four.
Common report language: new bones, stopped dreaming, name recall failure, bulb stage, root stage, sprout stage, vessel conversion, mockery formation.
Current location data: North Sector tree line, abandoned storm shelter three, old lake cabins, culvert access path B, and the south foundation corridor.

## Nature's Mockery
APSO-NM-011
Name: Nature's Mockery
Status: Active observation.
Category: Late-stage Deep Root manifestation.
Known associations: Deep Root Disease, Roots, The Iris, repeated speech, plant-like body mass, living victim persistence.
Activity summary: Nature's Mockery instances are recorded as dangerous plant-like or fungus-like bodies connected to final-stage Deep Root infection. Public lore records describe contact effects including hallucination, muscular paralysis, body disfigurement, and flesh decay. Some records describe victims as conscious during late-stage conversion. Instances may repeat sentences spoken to them rather than generate original speech.
Primary uncertainty: Some records imply an occupant or secondary organism may emerge from the interior of a mature instance, but the nature of the occupant is unconfirmed.
Current location data: wooded ditches, overgrown drainage areas, empty bedrooms with soil intrusion, derelict greenhouses, and rooms where vines are present without a known root source.
Common report language: plant not plant, copied vegetation, speaking shrub, voice from the ditch, hollow leaves, face in the root mass.

## Host of Influence
APSO-VC-HOST-002
Name: Host of Influence / Imperium Carnis
Status: Active spore-monitoring watch.
Category: Vita Carnis rooted organism, behavioral influence hazard.
Known associations: spores, altered coordination, directional walking, electromagnetic sensing, Nutrire-linked materials.
Activity summary: Vita Carnis lore describes the Host of Influence as a semi-humanoid organism rooted into the ground by fibrous tissue and tendrils. It can release spores that alter behavior after inhalation. Lore records describe restlessness, sluggish movement, numb joints, poor coordination, dizziness, migraines, impaired speech, tremors, and eventual movement toward the Host within several hours.
Primary uncertainty: Artemis records classify all Host spore data as fictional reference and not as real medical guidance.
Current location data: empty lots with fine orange dust, abandoned gardens, wind-exposed roads, low fields after sunset, and indoor rooms where powder appears near vents.
Common report language: orange dust, walking north without reason, knees locked, jaw shaking, voice slurring, no memory of leaving.

## Public Health And Epidemiology Reference
These entries are real terminology references used to give fictional Artemis reports more credible vocabulary. They are not medical instructions.

Term: Incubation period.
Definition: time between exposure to an infectious disease and the start of symptoms.
Artemis fictional equivalent: delay between contact with a hazard and first observable anomaly.

Term: Exposure.
Definition: contact with a hazard, infectious agent, contaminated surface, vector, animal, food, water, respiratory droplets, or other source.
Artemis fictional equivalent: contact, proximity, signal exposure, spore inhalation, touch event, voice-response event, or unexplained environmental contact.

Term: Symptom onset.
Definition: moment when signs or symptoms begin after exposure.
Artemis fictional equivalent: first report of physical change, memory change, movement change, voice repetition, dream loss, or abnormal behavior.

Term: Quarantine.
Definition: separation and restriction of movement for people who may have been exposed but are not known to be ill.
Artemis fictional equivalent: holding zone, observation status, exposure watch, advisory perimeter.

Term: Isolation.
Definition: separation of a person known or suspected to be infected or ill from those who are not infected.
Artemis fictional equivalent: confirmed hazard separation, sealed room, inactive wing, or contained signal area.

Term: Transmission route.
Definition: the pathway by which a disease agent or hazard moves from source to host.
Reference categories: direct contact, indirect contact, droplet, airborne, vector-borne, foodborne, waterborne, environmental exposure.
Artemis fictional equivalents: touch, root contact, spore inhalation, signal exposure, voice lure, visual lure, soil contamination, structure breach.

Term: Surveillance.
Definition: systematic collection, analysis, and interpretation of health or hazard data.
Artemis fictional equivalent: signal logs, public reports, sensor readings, tape archive review, incident registry.

Term: Cluster.
Definition: grouping of cases in time, place, or shared exposure.
Artemis fictional equivalent: multiple residents reporting the same voice, growth, dream loss, motion pattern, or station tone.

Term: Case classification.
Common categories: suspected, probable, confirmed, cleared, unresolved.
Artemis fictional equivalents: unverified signal, probable anomaly, confirmed advisory, false positive, dormant file.

Term: Risk communication.
Definition: exchange of information about risks during an emergency.
Artemis fictional equivalent: public alert, broadcast advisory, emergency recording, station bulletin.
